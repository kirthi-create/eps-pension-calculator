PENSION RESULT
Total Monthly Pension (₹)
Exact workbook formula:
Pension = INT[(B18 × B12) + (B19 × B11)] ÷ (70 × 365)
This mobile calculator reproduces the formulas in the supplied workbook. The EPFO portal's official result can differ if the underlying official records differ.
